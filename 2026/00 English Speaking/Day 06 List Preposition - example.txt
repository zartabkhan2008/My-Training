📘 Prepositions with Reason (Easy English)

🔹 IN / ON / UNDER (Place)

I am in the room
👉 Reason: “in” = inside something
The book is on the table
👉 Reason: “on” = touching surface
The cat is under the chair
👉 Reason: “under” = below something
The pen is in my bag
👉 Reason: inside bag
I sit on the chair
👉 Reason: surface (chair)
The dog is under the table
👉 Reason: below table
She is in the kitchen
👉 Reason: inside room
The phone is on the bed
👉 Reason: on surface
He is near me
👉 Reason: close distance
The school is near my house
👉 Reason: not far

🔹 AT / ON / IN (Time)

I wake up at 7
👉 Reason: exact time
I go to school at 8
👉 Reason: exact time
I study at night
👉 Reason: general time
I go to school on Monday
👉 Reason: day
We meet on Sunday
👉 Reason: day
My birthday is in June
👉 Reason: month
I was born in 2010
👉 Reason: year
I play in the evening
👉 Reason: part of day
I eat lunch at 1
👉 Reason: exact time
I watch TV at night
👉 Reason: fixed time expression

🔹 TO / FROM (Movement)

I go to school
👉 Reason: direction (going somewhere)
She goes to market
👉 Reason: direction
He comes from office
👉 Reason: starting point
I come from home
👉 Reason: where I start
We go to park
👉 Reason: destination
They come from school
👉 Reason: origin
I travel to Mumbai
👉 Reason: place you go
He returns from Delhi
👉 Reason: coming back

🔹 WITH / FOR / OF

I talk with my friend
👉 Reason: together
This gift is for you
👉 Reason: purpose
A cup of tea
👉 Reason: shows relation
I go with my brother
👉 Reason: company
She works for money
👉 Reason: reason/purpose
This is a photo of my family
👉 Reason: shows belonging
He is angry with me
👉 Reason: feeling toward someone

🔹 BEFORE / AFTER / DURING

I eat before school
👉 Reason: earlier time
I sleep after dinner
👉 Reason: later time
I study during class
👉 Reason: in the middle of time
She comes after lunch
👉 Reason: later
He left before me
👉 Reason: earlier

🔹 INTO / ONTO / OUT OF

He jumps into water
👉 Reason: outside → inside
The cat jumps onto the table
👉 Reason: movement to surface
She goes out of the room
👉 Reason: inside → outside
I walk into class
👉 Reason: enter
He climbs onto roof
👉 Reason: go up to surface

🔹 BY / ABOUT

I travel by bus
👉 Reason: method
The book is written by him
👉 Reason: doer
We talk about school
👉 Reason: topic
She reads about history
👉 Reason: subject/topic
I pay by card
👉 Reason: method
⚡ Easy Trick to Remember
In = inside
On = surface
At = exact
To = going
From = coming
With = together
For = purpose
