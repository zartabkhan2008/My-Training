📘 List of Subjects (Pronouns)

🔹 1. Singular (one person/thing)
I → میں
You → تم / آپ
He → وہ (لڑکا)
She → وہ (لڑکی)
It → یہ / وہ (چیز یا جانور)

👉 Examples:

I am a student
He plays cricket
She is happy

🔹 2. Plural (more than one)

We → ہم
You → تم لوگ / آپ لوگ
They → وہ لوگ

👉 Examples:

We are friends
They are playing
You are learning English
