📘 What is a Preposition?

👉 A preposition shows place, time, or position

Simple meaning:
➡️ It tells where, when, or how

📍 1.   of Place (where?)
In → اندر
On → اوپر
Under → نیچے
Between → کے درمیان
Among → کے درمیان (group)
Near → قریب
Far → دور
Behind → پیچھے
In front of → سامنے
Next to → کے پاس

👉 Examples:

The book is on the table
The cat is under the chair
He is near me
⏰ 2. Prepositions of Time (when?)
At → (exact time)
On → (day/date)
In → (month/year)
Before → پہلے
After → بعد
During → کے دوران
For → کے لئے (duration)
Since → سے (starting time)

👉 Examples:

I wake up at 7
I go to school on Monday
I was born in 2010
🔄 3. Prepositions of Movement
To → کی طرف
Into → کے اندر
Onto → کے اوپر
From → سے
Out of → باہر
Across → پار

👉 Examples:

I go to school
He jumped into water
She came from home
📊 Most Common Prepositions
Of → کا / کی / کے
For → کے لئے
With → کے ساتھ
About → کے بارے میں
By → کے ذریعے

👉 Examples:

This is a book of English
I am talking with my friend
This is a gift for you
⚡ Easy Formula

👉 Subject + verb + preposition + object

Example:

I sit on the chair
She is in the room
❌ Common Mistakes

❌ I am in Monday
✅ I am on Monday

❌ I go in school
✅ I go to school
