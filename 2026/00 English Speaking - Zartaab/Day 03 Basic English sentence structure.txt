🌱 Step 1: Basic Grammar (Foundation)

First learn these 3 things:

1. Subject (Who?)
I, You, He, She, It, We, They

2. Verb (Action)

eat, go, play, study, run

3. Object (What?) 

food, school, cricket, book

👉 Structure:

Subject + Verb + Object

Examples:

I eat food
I play cricket
She reads a book

🌱 Step 2: Learn “Be” verbs (Very Important)

I am
He/She/It is
You/We/They are

Examples:

I am a student
She is happy
They are playing

🌱 Step 3: Tenses (Start simple)

🔵 1. Present Tense (Daily things)

👉 Structure:
Subject + verb

Examples:

I eat food
I go to school
She plays cricket

🟡 2. Past Tense (Already happened)

👉 Structure:
Subject + verb (2nd form)

Examples:

I ate food
I went to school
I played cricket

🔴 3. Future Tense (Will happen)


👉 Structure:
Subject + will + verb

Examples:

I will eat food
I will go to school
I will play cricket

🌱 Step 4: Continuous Tense

Present Continuous

👉 am/is/are + verb + ing

I am eating
She is playing
They are studying
Past Continuous

👉 was/were + verb + ing

I was eating
They were playing
Future Continuous

👉 will be + verb + ing

I will be studying
🌱 Step 5: Perfect Tense (Little advanced)
Present Perfect

👉 has/have + verb (3rd form)

I have eaten
She has gone
Past Perfect

👉 had + verb (3rd form)

I had eaten
Future Perfect

👉 will have + verb (3rd form)

I will have finished
🌱 Step 6: Questions

👉 Use:

What, Where, Why, When, How

Examples:

What are you doing?
Where do you live?
Why are you sad?

Do this daily:
