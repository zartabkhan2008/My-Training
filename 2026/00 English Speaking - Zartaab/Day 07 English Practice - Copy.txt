   👉 Present = abhi + habit                           
👉 Past = koi bhi beet chuka time
👉 Future = koi bhi aane wala time
 
1) Present Simple
roz / habit / daily ka kaam
Formula: Subject + V1 (s/es)
Sentence: I play cricket
Meaning: Main roz cricket khelta hoon

2) Present Continuous
abhi chal raha hai (now)
Formula: Subject + am/is/are + ing
Sentence: I am playing cricket
Meaning: Main abhi cricket khel raha hoon

3)  Present Perfect
kar liya hai / chuka hoon ==> Subject + has/have + V3
rule ==> I / You / We / They ==> have       He / She / It ==> has

4) Present Perfect Continuous
👉 Jo kaam past me start hua tha aur abhi bhi chal raha hai
👉 usko kehte hain Present Perfect Continuous
Formula : Subject + have/has been + ing
rule ==> I / You / We / They ==> have       He / She / It ==> has

5) Past Simple 
jo kaam past main hogaya hoon ==> Subject + V2
I played cricket
maine cricket khela

6) Past Perfect
chuka tha  ==> Subject + had + V3

7) Past Continuous
raha thaa ==>  Subject + was/were + ing

8) Past Perfect Continuous Formula
Formula: Subject + had been + ing +    ( when using ==> kitne time se chal raha tha )
I had been studying for 2 hours
👉 main 2 ghante se padh raha tha

9) Future Simple Tense
khelunga ==> Subject + will + V1

10) Future Continuous Tense
raha hunga ==> Subject + will be + ing

1) in             andar/me                  I am in the room. — main andar hoon         She lives in Delhi. — wo Delhi me rehti hai
