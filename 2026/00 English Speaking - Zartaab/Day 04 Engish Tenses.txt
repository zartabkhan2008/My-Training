There are 3 main tenses:
Present
Past
Future

Each has 4 types → Total = 12 tenses

🟢 PRESENT TENSE (4 types)
1. Simple Present

👉 Subject + V1+Object

I eat food
She plays cricket
2. Present Continuous

👉 am/is/are + V1 + ing

I am eating
She is playing
3. Present Perfect

👉 has/have + V3

I have eaten
She has gone
4. Present Perfect Continuous

👉 has/have been + V1 + ing

I have been studying
She has been playing
🔵 PAST TENSE (4 types)
5. Simple Past

👉 Subject + V2

I ate food
I went to school
6. Past Continuous

👉 was/were + V1 + ing

I was eating
They were playing
7. Past Perfect

👉 had + V3

I had eaten
She had gone
8. Past Perfect Continuous

👉 had been + V1 + ing

I had been studying
They had been playing
🔴 FUTURE TENSE (4 types)
9. Simple Future

👉 will + V1

I will eat
I will go
10. Future Continuous

👉 will be + V1 + ing

I will be studying
11. Future Perfect

👉 will have + V3

I will have finished
12. Future Perfect Continuous

👉 will have been + V1 + ing

I will have been studying
